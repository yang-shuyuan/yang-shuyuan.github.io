I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Epidemiology at the Brown University School of Public Health. I hold a PhD in Public Health and an MS in Epidemiology and Biostatistics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and a Bachelor of Medicine in Preventive Medicine from Xiangya School of Medicine, Central South University.

My research focuses on **pharmacoepidemiology, real-world evidence (RWE), health economics and outcomes research (HEOR), and health technology assessment (HTA)**. I apply epidemiologic, statistical, and health economic methods to generate rigorous evidence on the effectiveness, safety, and value of medical treatments, with a particular emphasis on cardiometabolic, kidney, and liver diseases. My work spans the full evidence-generation process, from research question development and study design to data collection, statistical and economic analyses, evidence synthesis, and scientific communication, to inform clinical practice, healthcare policy, and decision-making.

## Research Areas

- **Pharmacoepidemiology:** Medication utilization, effectiveness, safety, and treatment strategies.
- **Real-World Evidence (RWE):** Evidence generation using electronic health records, administrative claims, and longitudinal observational data.
- **Health Economics and Outcomes Research (HEOR):** Clinical and economic outcomes, healthcare resource utilization, costs, and economic evaluation.
- **Health Technology Assessment (HTA):** Evidence synthesis, cost-effectiveness, and value assessment to inform healthcare decisions.

## Methodological Expertise

- Causal inference and target trial emulation
- Longitudinal and time-to-event analysis
- Propensity score methods and inverse probability weighting
- Clone-censor-weighting and treatment strategy evaluation
- Economic evaluation and decision-analytic modeling
- Systematic reviews, meta-analysis, and evidence synthesis
- Statistical programming and data analysis using SAS and R
